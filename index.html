    FAQ

    Good
    questions.

    Will it work with my phone?

    Yes. Every powerbank has built-in USB-C, Lightning and Micro-USB cables, so it charges iPhone, Android, earbuds and most handheld devices.

    Do I need an app?

    No. Every station has a card reader. Insert or tap a debit or credit card, or pay with Apple Pay or Google Pay, and a slot unlocks.

    Can I return it somewhere else?

    Yes, that's the idea. Push it into any Charg-nz station with a free slot and the rental stops straight away.

    What if the station is full when I return?

    The station finder shows free slots. If you arrive and every slot is taken, contact us and we'll pause your rental while you find the next one.

    What if I lose it?

    If a powerbank isn't returned within 24 hours, a one-off NZ$30 charge applies instead of further rental fees.
